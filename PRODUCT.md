# KECHOO Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are overseas industrial end users and distributors across Europe, North America, Southeast Asia, and other core bandsaw-blade markets. End users need to identify a blade that fits their machine, workpiece, and cutting objective, then either purchase an in-stock standard size or request technical advice and a quote. Distributors need clear product ranges, dependable specifications, OEM and bulk-order information, and a direct route to commercial cooperation.

## Product Purpose

Build an English-first WordPress and WooCommerce independent site for KECHOO (奇楚), a bandsaw-blade brand serving food and bone processing, woodworking, and metal cutting. The site must function as both a technical product-selection resource and a sales channel: standard in-stock products can be purchased online, while custom sizes, complex applications, and wholesale requirements flow into a structured RFQ process. Success means qualified inquiries, completed stock-product orders, distributor leads, and measurable organic-search growth.

## Brand Personality

Professional, reliable, and pragmatic. The English brand line is **“KECHOO — Choose Better Cutting.”** The Chinese brand promise is **“工艺出奇，品质出众。”** The brand should communicate manufacturing competence through accurate specifications, application evidence, quality-control details, and clear commercial terms. Its voice is direct and useful, with enough technical depth for experienced buyers and enough guidance for customers who need help selecting a blade. Innovation is expressed through better cutting outcomes, not abstract technology claims.

## Anti-references

Avoid a crowded marketplace-style homepage, vague factory claims, unedited or awkward English, and a lowest-price-only position. Do not mix food, wood, and metal applications into one undifferentiated catalog. Avoid decorative industrial cliches, excessive animation, generic stock-factory imagery, and product pages that force buyers to interpret hundreds of raw variations without selection guidance.

## Design Principles

1. Organize around the cutting job first: lead visitors into food and bone, wood, or metal applications before asking them to understand blade manufacturing terminology.
2. Make technical confidence visible: specifications, compatibility, test evidence, quality control, and documentation should carry more weight than slogans.
3. Give every buyer a clear next action: purchase a verified in-stock configuration, request a custom quote, or ask for selection help.
4. Separate audiences without splitting the brand: serve end users through selection and checkout, and distributors through bulk, OEM, and partnership paths.
5. Keep product data structured and reusable so filtering, comparison, SEO pages, RFQs, and future languages share one dependable source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the practical baseline even though no special accessibility requirement was requested. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient color contrast, meaningful alternative text, color-independent status communication, clear form errors, and reduced-motion preferences. English is the launch language; the information architecture and product data must remain ready for future regional languages.
